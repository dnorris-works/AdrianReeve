Deep Field Press

Deep Field Press is an independent publishing company founded in 2025, dedicated to producing thoughtful fiction in both eBook and print formats.

What We Publish

We publish fiction that explores consciousness, identity, and the questions most people are afraid to sit with. Our catalog focuses on literary and speculative works that reward reflection.

Where to Find Us

Deep Field Press titles are currently available through Amazon KDP in both eBook and paperback formats. Distribution may expand to additional platforms in the future.

About the Company

Deep Field Press is a sole-proprietor publishing imprint. We are not currently accepting outside submissions.
